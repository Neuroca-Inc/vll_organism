# CEG Integration into the VLL Mechanism Ladder v0.1

## Decision

Preserve the completed Counterfactual Echo Gain (CEG) experiment as a later **actionable internal-state benchmark** for the VLL/VDM reconstruction program.

The immediate R1a task remains graph-local CF04-derived flux relaxation. CEG changes the future benchmark plan, not the current mechanism choice.

## Established result

Under the preregistered metriplectic assisted-echo protocol, a model-aware assistance micro-sequence produced a statistically positive counterfactual echo gain while satisfying the experiment's physics gates and energy-match constraint.

Reported preregistered result:

- median CEG = 0.054552
- lambda = 0.5
- n = 12 seeds
- J-Noether gate passed
- M-monotonicity / H-theorem gate passed
- Strang defect QC passed
- energy parity retained

The narrow capability statement is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{retained model structure contained actionable information that improved a constrained future trajectory}
}
\]

## Why this matters

Rung-0 VLL tests passive routing:

\[
\text{perturbation} \rightarrow \text{propagation} \rightarrow \text{destination structure}.
\]

CEG tests a different capability:

\[
\text{perturbation}
\rightarrow
\text{internal structural knowledge}
\rightarrow
\text{bounded counterfactual assistance}
\rightarrow
\text{improved recovery}.
\]

That makes CEG useful when Cortex walkers/maps become more than observers and acquire a legitimate bounded action channel.

## B-CEG benchmark

Once a rung contains:

1. an admitted dynamical field/state;
2. retained local walker/map observations of that field;
3. a bounded action channel that can alter a future local trajectory;

run a matched-control assisted-echo test.

Primary comparison:

\[
\text{blind / unassisted control}
\quad\text{vs}\quad
\text{state-aware bounded assistance}.
\]

Required controls:

- equal work / energy parity;
- invariant-preservation gates appropriate to the rung;
- preregistered assistance budget;
- blind or shuffled-state assistance null;
- no direct access to the future evaluation target;
- deterministic provenance and replay.

## Mechanism-ladder discriminator

A future Cortex coupling does not receive capability credit merely because maps correlate with useful structure.

It earns a stronger result only if:

\[
\boxed{
\text{map/walker state}
\rightarrow
\text{bounded local intervention}
\rightarrow
\text{measurable improvement over matched controls}
}
\]

This distinguishes **descriptive state** from **actionable causal state**.

## Relationship to CF04

CF04 and CEG answer different questions.

CF04-derived R1a:

\[
\text{Does retained flux memory change transport in the predicted way?}
\]

B-CEG:

\[
\text{Can retained internal dynamical knowledge be used to choose a better constrained trajectory?}
\]

Do not merge them into one rung.

Current sequence:

\[
V_0
\rightarrow
\text{R1a graph flux relaxation}
\rightarrow
\text{R1b semantic replay}
\rightarrow
\text{later admitted Cortex observation/coupling}
\rightarrow
\boxed{\text{B-CEG actionable-state test}}
\]

The exact rung number for B-CEG remains open until the mechanism ancestry establishes when a legitimate action channel first exists.
